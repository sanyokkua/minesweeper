Settings

Input mode
Choose what a tap does and what a long-press / right-click does. Applies everywhere, on touch and desktop.
Tap to reveal · Hold to flag
Default. Quick tap opens a cell; long-press or right-click places a flag.
Tap to flag · Hold to reveal
Quick tap places a flag; long-press or right-click opens the cell.
Theme
Light and dark are both built from the same palette.
Local data
Settings and best times are stored only in this browser.

How to play

1
Reveal a cell
Shows a number for how many mines touch it, or clears a whole empty region if it touches none.
2
Flag a cell
Mark a suspected mine. Flagged cells can't be opened by accident.
3
Two actions, your choice
In Settings, pick whether tap reveals and hold flags, or the reverse — same rule for mouse and touch.
✕
Win or lose
Reveal every non-mine cell to win. Reveal a mine and the round ends immediately.
